    <h1>Glasgow Prestwick Airport Parking Guide</h1>

    <p>
      Planning ahead can make airport parking simpler and help you compare the
      options available for your travel dates. Consider the parking location,
      transfer time, security features and the full cost before booking.
    </p>

    <h2>How Park and Ride Parking Works</h2>

    <p>
      With park and ride parking, you drive to an off-airport car park, check in,
      park your vehicle and take a shuttle transfer to the airport terminal.
      This can be a practical option for holidays, business trips and longer stays.
    </p>

    <h2>What to Check Before You Book</h2>

    <ul>
      <li>Whether the car park has suitable security measures.</li>
      <li>The expected shuttle transfer time to the terminal.</li>
      <li>Whether online booking is available for your travel dates.</li>
      <li>The full price and any booking terms before payment.</li>
      <li>Return instructions for collecting your vehicle after your flight.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>Travel Tip</h2>
